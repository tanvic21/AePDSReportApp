How to install (4 steps)

1

First delete the OLD app

Press and hold the old app icon, then tap Uninstall / Delete.

2

Tap the yellow DOWNLOAD button

Wait till the file finishes downloading.

3

Open the file and tap INSTALL

If the phone asks, tap "Settings" and allow, then go back and tap Install.

4

Open the app

Find the app icon on your phone and tap it.

How to use the app

1

Log in

Type your shop number and your license key. Ask the person who gave you the app if you don't have it.

2

Choose the report

Monthly, Daily, or pick your own dates.

3

Get your PDF

Save it or send it on WhatsApp.
